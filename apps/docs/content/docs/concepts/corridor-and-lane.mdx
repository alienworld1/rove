A **Corridor** is one business relationship between an e-mobility service provider (**eMSP**, the payer) and a charge point operator (**CPO**, the payee). Use it to review their charging sessions together: verified delivery, protected payment, collected payment, and possible exposure. Its payment asset, tariff, exposure limits, reserve settings, and settlement policy apply to each new Lane.

## Independent ownership

A **Lane** is one charging session, with its own meter evidence, payment channel, and reserve. Select a Lane to inspect why its protection changed and whether its final totals match.

For integration, each Lane binds one external charging session, charger, allowed meter identity, maximum power in watts, expected proof interval in milliseconds, and one channel. It retains its own version, evidence freshness, authorization, settlement, confirmed deposit, exposure bound, and completion record.

The three controlled Lanes in the built-in Lab scenario share a Corridor but have separate reserves and channels. A fault in Lane C does not pool or borrow Lane A/B's protection. Aggregate Corridor projections summarize the individual Lanes; they do not create spendable funding or change per-Lane authority.

## State and selection

- **Initializing:** waiting for verified readings and funded authorization.
- **Protected:** current evidence and payment protection allow operation within policy.
- **Watch / At risk:** reserve or exposure needs attention; inspect the recorded reason before the limit is reached.
- **Pause required:** charging must pause until protection is restored.
- **Recovering:** Rove is checking whether evidence and payment protection permit resumption.
- **Completing:** final evidence, accounting, or payment is still being checked.
- **Reconciled:** final totals match; channel closure and reserve return remain separate steps.
- **Closed:** channel closure and the unused reserve return have been verified, including a zero return when no reserve remains.

The persisted states are `INITIALIZING`, `PROTECTED`, `WATCH`, `AT_RISK`, `PAUSE_REQUIRED`, `RECOVERING`, `COMPLETING`, `RECONCILED`, and `CLOSED`. Current state, causal history, and historical trace selection are different views of the same retained records. Selecting an event sends no financial command.

The canonical types are in `packages/domain/src/index.ts`; browser presentation contracts are in `packages/contracts/src/corridor.ts`. [System overview](/docs/architecture/system-overview) explains persistence and projections. [Protection window](/docs/concepts/protection-window) defines the values that must remain independent.

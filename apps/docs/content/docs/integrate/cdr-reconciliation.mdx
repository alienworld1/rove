The narrow OCPI-style adapter in `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts` accepts a concluded-session energy-only tax-free record. It follows relevant [OCPI CDR concepts](https://github.com/ocpi/ocpi/blob/2.3.0/release/core/mod_cdrs.asciidoc); it does not implement full OCPI transport or claim protocol conformance.

## Binding and exact precision

Session, source party, meter, charger, start/end times, currency, settlement asset mapping, and tariff must agree with authenticated session context. `totalEnergyKwh` and `totalCost` are decimal strings, preserving source text and spelling. Energy normalizes exactly to whole Wh (three kWh fractional places, with additional trailing zeroes allowed); cost normalizes exactly to configured asset base units. Sub-Wh, nonzero unsupported fractional units, numeric JSON decimals, extra fields, FX, tax, time, parking, and fixed charges are rejected.

Server settings explicitly declare `CDR_CURRENCY`, `CDR_SETTLEMENT_ASSET_ID`, and `CDR_SOURCE_PARTY_ID`. Local simulation uses USD / TEST_ASSET / controlled-cpo. A six-decimal pathUSD context declares direct USD equivalence, not currency conversion.

## Safe accounting example

This versioned record normalizes to 1000 Wh and 500000 base units when bound to the matching six-decimal context. It is a controlled example, not a live session.

```json
{
  "externalCdrId": "record-123",
  "externalSessionId": "session-123",
  "sourcePartyId": "controlled-cpo",
  "meterId": "meter-123",
  "chargerId": "charger-123",
  "startedAt": "2026-10-03T12:00:00Z",
  "endedAt": "2026-10-03T12:01:00Z",
  "currency": "USD",
  "totalEnergyKwh": "1.000",
  "totalCost": "0.500000",
  "tariffBasis": {
    "perKwh": "500000",
    "tariffDecimals": 6,
    "assetDecimals": 6
  },
  "chargeType": "energy_only_tax_free"
}
```

## Immutable delivery

The engine retains original source text and SHA-256 content hash. Identical content under the same provider record ID is harmless; changed content conflicts. Records cannot be edited to force a match. `/api/lanes/:id/cdr` accepts authenticated external ingestion using the strict Lane command envelope; browser completion accepts only identity/version/command intent.

## Four totals and two energies

Compare final trusted service value, final authorized value, final settled value, and normalized accounting total exactly. Compare final accepted meter Wh with CDR Wh. Deltas are signed integer strings: accounting, authorization, and settlement minus trusted value, and CDR energy minus accepted energy. Any nonzero difference is `MISMATCH`, not a correction or rounding allowance.

## Finish and close

After the controlled service trace stops, focus its Lane and choose **Finish session**. The server supplies final signed evidence and its immutable fixture, authorizes remaining trusted value, and requests final settlement. **Check again** inspects existing work. Pending operations continue after navigation. **Close channel** becomes available only when matching totals, accepted final evidence, fresh authority, and resolved earlier work permit it.

Zero-service totals and zero-refund closure must be verified, not confused with absent values. The canonical close receipt and exact returned reserve are retained after refresh/restart. A mismatch remains visible and blocks closure. Read [final settlement and refund proof](/docs/integrate/tempo/channel-lifecycle#close-and-refund) and [failure recovery](/docs/architecture/failure-and-recovery).

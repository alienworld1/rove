Reasons explain rejection, protection, completion, or adapter validation. A reason is not itself a command or a guarantee that retrying is safe. The inventory below classifies exact current names and links each to its source.

If your reason is not listed, inspect the selected event’s evidence and payment status. The app links unlisted reasons to this page; an unlisted reason does not establish that protection is restored or that a payment is safe to retry.

## Evidence signature invalid

`EVIDENCE_SIGNATURE_INVALID` means the original signed meter data failed P-256 / SHA-256 verification against the configured key. Rejected evidence cannot advance trusted billable value or authorization. Preserve the original bytes, authenticate the correct key/source/session, and submit valid evidence with expected continuity. Do not edit the data while retaining its signature.

Follow the cause to the contract: [evidence verification](/docs/integrate/meter-evidence) → [conservative exposure bound](/docs/concepts/protection-window) → [verified recovery](/docs/architecture/failure-and-recovery).

Source: `packages/evidence/src/index.ts` → `verifyOcmf`.

## Protection, completion, and adapter reasons

### cdr accounting mismatch

`CDR_ACCOUNTING_MISMATCH` — Accounting total differs from trusted service value. Preserve the immutable input; closure remains blocked.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr authorization mismatch

`CDR_AUTHORIZATION_MISMATCH` — Final authorization differs from trusted service. Inspect final voucher and funding before proceeding.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr binding invalid

`CDR_BINDING_INVALID` — Adapter/transition validation. Accounting identity or currency differs from authenticated context. Reject this input; authenticate the correct source.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr currency mapping missing

`CDR_CURRENCY_MAPPING_MISSING` — Adapter/transition validation. Server has no explicit currency/asset mapping. Configure it before accepting accounting; no FX is inferred.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr energywh mismatch

`CDR_ENERGYWH_MISMATCH` — CDR Wh differs from final accepted meter Wh. Retain the mismatch; there is no force-match action.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr missing

`CDR_MISSING` — Completion awaits immutable accounting input. Obtain the bound final record.

Source: `packages/protection/src/completion.ts`.

### cdr precision unsupported

`CDR_PRECISION_UNSUPPORTED` — Adapter/transition validation. Nonzero fractional units cannot normalize exactly. Reject the record rather than rounding it.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr service mismatch

`CDR_SERVICE_MISMATCH` — Final accounting energy or cost differs from trusted service. Keep the mismatch visible and block close.

Source: `packages/protection/src/completion.ts`.

### cdr session time invalid

`CDR_SESSION_TIME_INVALID` — Adapter/transition validation. Accounting times do not match service context or valid chronology. Reject the input.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr settlement mismatch

`CDR_SETTLEMENT_MISMATCH` — Captured cumulative value differs from final trusted value. Inspect settlement authority; closure is blocked.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr tariff unsupported

`CDR_TARIFF_UNSUPPORTED` — Adapter/transition validation. Accounting tariff differs from the bound fixed tariff. Reject the input; do not reprice service.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

### cdr too large

`CDR_TOO_LARGE` — Adapter/transition validation. Raw accounting exceeds the adapter byte bound. Reject delivery; use the narrow supported record.

Source: `packages/ocpi/src/index.ts`.

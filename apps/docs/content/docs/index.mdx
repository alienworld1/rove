title: "What is Rove?"
description: "Understand payment protection for EV roaming, explore a charging session, and plan your integration."
---

Rove helps charging operators limit the payment risk of serving another mobility provider’s customers. Signed meter readings establish the value of electricity delivered. The mobility provider funds a small rolling reserve and authorizes payment as charging progresses. Rove tracks how much service could remain unprotected and requires a pause when that exposure reaches the agreed limit.

This guide is for **finance and roaming operators** at charging and mobility businesses, and for **engineers** connecting their charging, payment, and accounting systems. Drivers do not use Rove or connect a wallet.

- **How much is protected, collected, or still at risk?** Read [the protection window](/docs/concepts/protection-window).
- **How do I try it?** Open a recorded example from Rove’s home page, or use an invite to [run your own scenario in Rove Lab](/docs/lab).

## The terms you will see

A **Corridor** is one business relationship between the mobility provider that pays and the charging operator that receives payment. A **Lane** is one charging session within that relationship, with its own payment channel and reserve.

**Protected value** is verified charging value backed by funded payment authorization: it can be collected under the channel’s rules. **Settled value** is payment already collected. The **exposure bound** is a conservative estimate of service value that could exceed protection; it is not a bill or an exact meter reading.

## Explore Rove without setup

On Rove’s home page, choose **Explore a recorded example** or browse **Recorded examples**. Select a Lane or event to inspect its meter reading, payment record, and protection decision. **Play recording** and the timeline let you review past states; viewing a recording never starts a payment.

Choose **Have an invite?** to open **Your workspace**. You can **Run on Tempo testnet** using the built-in three-session example, or open **Scenario builder** to create your own. Rove supplies test funds; you do not need to connect a wallet. Charging equipment is simulated. Tempo receipts identify real activity on the test network, not production payments.

## What is supported today?

Rove currently supports a fixed energy tariff, a specific signed OCMF meter format, an OCPI-style final accounting record, and the configured Tempo payment channel. Final accounting compares verified energy, billable value, authorized payment, and collected payment before closure returns unused reserve.

A production integration needs authenticated meter readings, approved meter keys, payment signers and funding, and a charging management system that enforces Rove’s pause requirement. Rove does not provide a full roaming platform, physical charger controller, driver billing, tax calculation, currency conversion, or parking and time-based tariffs. Read [supported boundaries](/docs/reference/security) before planning an integration.

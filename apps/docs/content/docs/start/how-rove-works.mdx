Rove advances protection only when the evidence and payment boundaries agree. The browser displays the engine's persisted projection; it does not authorize money or decide risk.

## The operating loop

1. Bind the Lane to a Corridor, external session, charger, meter key, fixed tariff, power limit, and separate channel.
2. Parse and verify original signed meter bytes. Accepted cumulative readings become trusted Wh and integer billable value.
3. Confirm channel funding. A cooperating signer supplies a cumulative voucher no larger than trusted value or confirmed deposit.
4. Verify that authorization. Protected value is the lesser of trusted value and valid authorization; it is distinct from settlement.
5. Request rolling top-ups as headroom narrows. Only confirmed observations extend funded capacity. Partial settlement may capture protected value during service.
6. When evidence is untrusted/stale, grow a conservative upper bound. At the configured exposure boundary, require a pause. Rove emits a pause requirement. Your CPMS must enforce it on a physical charger.
7. Recover through accepted evidence, current channel truth, restored signing, and invariant checks. Recovery is not a UI reset.
8. Verify final signed evidence and immutable accounting, settle the final total, compare totals, and close with attributed reserve refund proof.

## Inspect the cause

The Corridor groups independent Lanes. An attached Inspector shows the evidence or payment cause behind an event. A rejection freezes trusted advancement, even while a digital twin's potential service bound grows. [Protection calculations](/docs/concepts/protection-window) explain that difference.

[Rove Lab](/docs/lab) reproduces the loop using controlled signed inputs and local payment simulation. The explicitly selected live CLI instead waits for Tempo confirmations. [Failure and recovery](/docs/architecture/failure-and-recovery) explains why an unknown outcome must be checked before retrying.
